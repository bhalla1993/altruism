Meet the Baker

Trained in classic French patisserie and inspired by family recipes, Elise brings a balance of precision and playfulness to every bake.

From laminated doughs to delicate meringues, each pastry is crafted by hand in our open kitchen, where you can watch the process from flour to final glaze.
