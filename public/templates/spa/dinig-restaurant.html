The Chef Behind the Curtain

Trained in Paris and seasoned in Michelin‑starred kitchens across Europe, Chef Julien Moreau brings a cinematic approach to fine dining—where every course is a scene, and every detail is intentional.

His cooking philosophy blends classical French technique with global influences, highlighting seasonal produce, sustainable sourcing, and unexpected flavor pairings.

  • Experience in multiple Michelin‑starred restaurants
  • Focus on seasonal, locally sourced ingredients
  • Dedicated vegetarian and pescatarian tasting menus
  • Personally curates each night’s menu progression

Wine & Pairings

Our sommelier‑curated wine program is designed to echo, contrast, and elevate each course—featuring old‑world classics, new‑world discoveries, and limited allocations.

Featured Cellar Highlights

  • Champagne, Grand Cru Blanc de Blancs
    Precision, minerality, and fine mousse—often poured to open the evening.
  • Burgundy, Premier Cru Pinot Noir
    Silky red fruit and earth, ideal with hearth‑roasted mains.
  • Napa Valley, Single‑Vineyard Cabernet
    Structured, dark, and powerful—reserved for special pairings and private dinners.
  • Sauternes, Late Harvest
    Honeyed stone fruit and balanced acidity, a natural partner for “Midnight Gold.”

Sommelier’s Philosophy

Our sommelier team believes in pairings that tell a story—sometimes in harmony, sometimes in contrast, always in service of the dish.

Non‑alcoholic pairings are available, featuring house‑made infusions, teas, and zero‑proof cocktails crafted with the same care as our wine program.
